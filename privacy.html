Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

256 TEAMS WC does not require an account and does not sell personal information. This page briefly explains the limited data used to run the site and private online rooms.

Who operates this site

256 TEAMS WC is operated by Mohamed A. For privacy questions or requests, contact the operator by Email.

What the site uses

  • Browser storage keeps your tournament, settings and preferences on your device. It is not sent to the operator and can be removed by resetting the tournament or clearing site data.
  • Online rooms temporarily store the display name you choose, your room membership, join time and room state. Access tokens are stored only in your current browser tab; the server stores a one-way hash rather than the token itself.
  • Cloudflare, Google Fonts and FlagCDN help host, secure and display the site and may receive normal technical request information under their own privacy policies.

This information is used only to provide, secure and improve the simulator. Providers may process technical information outside the UK under their own safeguards.

How long it is kept

Local tournament saves remain until you delete them. Online room access is removed from browser storage when the tab closes. A room is deleted when its host closes it or automatically after six hours.

Your rights

You may have rights over your personal information, including access, correction or deletion. Because there are no accounts and analytics are anonymous, it may not be possible to identify data linked to you. Contact the address above with a request. UK visitors may also contact the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if the site's features or data use changes.
